---
title: "Principal"
description: "Who acts: a verified sender, approver or operator."
icon: "Braces"
---

Every Thread method that appends takes one, for example the local operator &#123;issuer: "api", tenant: "local", subject: "operator"&#125;. In TypeScript, Principal is also the schema that parses one.

## Fields

<Field name="issuer" type={"string"} required>
  The identity authority that verified it, such as "api" or "slack:T024BE7LD". Non-empty.
</Field>

<Field name="tenant" type={"string"} required>
  The isolation scope: a principal may act only on threads of its own tenant. Non-empty.
</Field>

<Field name="subject" type={"string"} required>
  Who it is within the issuer, such as a user id. Non-empty.
</Field>
