A union discriminated by `status`.

### cancel_requested

<Field name="status" type={"\"cancel_requested\""} required>
  "cancel_requested": the cancel is accepted and durable, not yet applied; the member ends cancelled at its next step.
</Field>

<Field name="member" type={"MemberRef"} required>
  The member being cancelled.
</Field>

### refused

<Field name="status" type={"\"refused\""} required>
  "refused": nothing was done; code says why.
</Field>

<Field name="code" type={"CancelRefusal | OperatorRefusal"} required>
  Why it was refused.
</Field>
